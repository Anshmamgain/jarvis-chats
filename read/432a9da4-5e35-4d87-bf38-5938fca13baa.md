### Alfred  
**Alok Kumar** — CEO of **Cozmo AI** (YC W22, SF + Dubai, ~6 people). Forbes 30U30. Co-founder [Nuha Hashem](https://www.hellocozmo.ai/about-us); they ran a regulated fintech in Dubai together before this.

**Cozmo in kid terms, sir:**

Banks and insurance companies have huge rooms of people doing boring phone-and-paperwork jobs. "Did you pay your loan?" "Send me the photo of your crashed car." "Fill this form into our system."

Cozmo sells **robot employees** that do those jobs. Each one can:
- **Talk** to customers on the phone in real time
- **Look** at documents and videos (a photo of a dented car, an ID)
- **Think** using that company's own rulebook
- **Do** the actual work in the company's software (Salesforce, Guidewire, Temenos)

The trick: banks are heavily regulated, so a robot can't just guess. Cozmo's "Agent Protocol Engine" writes down *why* it decided each thing — a receipt trail for auditors. That's the moat.

Claimed traction: 10+ enterprises (incl. Nestlé), 1M+ production calls, ~70% cost cut. Backed by YC, Goodwater, Dubai Future District Fund.

So: **not a chatbot — an AI worker that closes the loop, sold to industries where "show your work" is the law.**

Sources: [YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/cozmo-ai) · [hellocozmo.ai](https://www.hellocozmo.ai/about-us) · [Wamda](https://www.wamda.com/2024/12/yc-backed-cozmox-ai-launches-voice-ai-employees-platform)
